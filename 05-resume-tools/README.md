# 05 — Resume Tools

Prompts, templates, and frameworks for tailoring your resume and cover letter to DS, DA, BA, and PA roles — without starting from scratch every time.

**Status: Planned**

---

## The Problem

Most resume advice is generic. "Use action verbs. Quantify your impact. Keep it to one page." You already know that.

The harder problem for DS/DA roles is:
- How do you describe ML work to a PM who doesn't know what collaborative filtering is?
- How do you tailor the same role for a "Data Scientist" JD versus a "Product Analyst" JD?
- How do you write a cover letter that doesn't sound like everyone else's cover letter?
- What do you cut when your experience doesn't match what a specific company is hiring for?

This folder is built to answer those questions, not the generic ones.

---

## Planned Contents

### Resume Optimizer
- **JD parsing prompt:** Extract the 5–7 most important signals from any job description
- **Gap analysis prompt:** Compare your resume against those signals and surface the real gaps
- **Bullet rewrite prompts:** For each experience bullet — surface the business impact, not just the technical method
- **Version control framework:** How to maintain a master resume and generate role-specific versions without losing track

### Cover Letter Templates
- **DS/MLE roles:** Lead with a specific technical decision, connect to the company's data challenges
- **DA/PA roles:** Lead with a business problem you solved, show how you structured the analysis
- **BA roles:** Lead with a stakeholder impact story, show the bridge between data and decision
- Each template is ~250 words — short enough to be read, specific enough to not be skipped

### Role-Specific Tailoring Guides
| Role | What They're Actually Looking For | What to Emphasize |
|------|----------------------------------|-------------------|
| Data Scientist | ML judgment + product understanding | Model selection reasoning, A/B test design, business impact of ML systems |
| Data Analyst | SQL + storytelling + structured thinking | Insight generation, how you communicated findings, metrics you owned |
| Business Analyst | Stakeholder influence + structured problem solving | Business decisions you shaped, frameworks you built, cross-functional work |
| Product Analyst | Product intuition + experimentation | Feature launches, experiments you designed/analyzed, north star metric thinking |

### Interview Story Bank
- A set of prompts to extract 8–10 strong stories from your experience
- Mapped to common behavioral questions (biggest impact, disagreed with a decision, worked with messy data, etc.)
- STAR formatting guide tailored to technical roles (where "Situation" should be brief and "Result" must be quantified)
