# 02 — Case Interview Casebook

40 case studies with full worked responses — organized by archetype, calibrated to the DS/DA interview bar at mid-to-large tech companies.

**Status: In Progress**

---

## What's Coming

The playbook in `01-case-study-playbook` teaches you the frameworks. This casebook is where you build the reps.

40 cases, each with:
- The full prompt (as it would appear in an interview)
- A worked response following the SPADE framework
- Annotated breakdown of what makes the response strong
- Common mistakes and how to avoid them for that case type
- 2–3 follow-up questions with model answers

---

## Planned Structure

Cases will be organized by the 7 archetypes from the playbook:

| Archetype | Cases | Notes |
|-----------|-------|-------|
| Metric Diagnosis | 12 | Highest volume — mirrors real interview distribution (~40-50% of cases) |
| Metric Definition | 6 | North Star, success metrics, guardrail design |

## How to Use This (Once Live)

Don't read all 40 cases. Practice them.

For each case:
1. Read the prompt. Close the file. Attempt a response out loud or in writing.
2. Open the worked response only after you've committed to your own answer.
3. Compare on the 5 axes: structure, business intuition, analytical reasoning, communication, technical depth.
4. Note the gaps. Do the same case again in 48 hours from memory.

The goal isn't to memorize responses. It's to build pattern recognition fast enough that structuring becomes automatic.

## Generation Approach

These will be generated using Claude with prompts that stress-test for:
- Realistic ambiguity (prompts that don't tell you the archetype)
- Conflicting signals
- Edge cases that require domain-specific knowledge
- Follow-up escalations that probe depth

The `prompts.md` in folder `01` includes notes on how to generate more cases yourself if you want to go beyond 40.
