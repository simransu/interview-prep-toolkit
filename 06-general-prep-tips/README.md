### Quick Heuristics (The Short List)
Things worth having memorized before any DS/DA interview:

- DAU = New + Retained + Resurrected − Churned (most people forget Resurrected)
- Revenue drops are usually Users or ARPU — decompose before hypothesizing
- When metrics conflict, check for Simpson's Paradox before concluding anything
- Data quality check takes 30 seconds and is almost always worth doing
- The first 60 seconds set your score more than the next 9 minutes
- Never ask the interviewer what they think the cause is
- A p-value of 0.08 is not "failure" — look at the confidence interval
- An SRM means you stop, investigate, and restart — never salvage the results
- For any growth question: identify the weakest AARRR stage before proposing solutions
- Edge cases win interviews — seasonality and mix shifts are table stakes; cohort maturation and notification suppression are not
